Chicken Biryani

Description

Chicken biryani is a classic one-pot layered rice dish made with marinated chicken, fragrant basmati rice, caramelized onions, and warm whole spices.

Slow-cooking the layered rice and spiced chicken on low heat (dum) locks in moisture and infuses every grain with rich aroma.

Ingredients

  • 500g bone-in chicken pieces
  • 2 cups aged basmati rice, soaked for 30 minutes
  • 1 cup plain yogurt (curd)
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tsp biryani masala powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • Whole spices: 2 bay leaves, 4 green cardamoms, 4 cloves, 1 cinnamon stick
  • A handful of fresh mint and coriander leaves, chopped
  • 3 tbsp cooking oil or ghee
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, red chili powder, biryani masala, half the chopped herbs, and salt for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot and fry the sliced onions until deep golden brown; remove half for garnishing.
  3. Add the marinated chicken and chopped tomatoes to the pot, cooking over medium heat for 12–15 minutes until the chicken is nearly tender and the gravy thickens.
  4. In a separate pot, boil water with salt and whole spices, then cook the soaked basmati rice until 70% done and drain thoroughly.
  5. Layer the parboiled rice evenly over the chicken gravy, topping with the reserved fried onions, remaining mint and coriander leaves, and a drizzle of ghee.
  6. Cover the pot tightly with a lid and cook on the lowest flame (dum) for 15–20 minutes, then let it rest for 5 minutes before fluffing and serving.
