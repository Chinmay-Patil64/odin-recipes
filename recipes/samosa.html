Punjabi Samosa

Description

Crispy, flaky golden pastries stuffed with a savory, spiced filling of mashed potatoes, green peas, and roasted aromatic spices.

Best served hot alongside tangy tamarind and fresh mint-coriander chutneys as an evening snack.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 4 tablespoons ghee or oil (plus extra for deep frying)
  • 1/2 teaspoon carom seeds (ajwain)
  • 4 medium potatoes, boiled, peeled, and coarsely mashed
  • 1/2 cup green peas, boiled
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 tablespoon ginger and green chili, finely minced
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon dry mango powder (amchur)
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • Fresh coriander leaves, finely chopped
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, carom seeds, salt, and ghee in a bowl until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs, then knead with cold water into a stiff dough. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a pan, crackle the cumin seeds, and sauté the minced ginger and green chilies for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the boiled peas, mashed potatoes, garam masala, amchur, red chili powder, and salt. Stir well for 3 to 4 minutes, mix in fresh coriander, and let the filling cool completely.
  4. Divide the rested dough into equal balls, roll each into an oval, and cut it in half to form two semicircles.
  5. Brush water along the straight edge of a semicircle, fold it into a cone, stuff with the potato filling, and pinch the top edge firmly to seal.
  6. Heat oil on medium-low heat and deep-fry the samosas in batches, turning occasionally until crisp and golden brown all over.
